From local project to published Task

  1. Prepare your project: define the model, training and evaluation functions, data requirements, and dependencies.
  2. Train locally: produce the Initiative Model and inspect the result.
  3. Check release readiness: validate the required project contracts and prepare a Release Candidate.
  4. Publish in FedOps Web: make the Task available in the Registry.
  5. Approve participants: review requests and configure your Campaign.
